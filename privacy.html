LEGAL & POLICIES Brides Right Hand

Privacy Policy.

LAST UPDATED: NOVEMBER 2026

At Brides Right Hand, owned and operated by Christina Haydanek, we honor the intimacy and significance of your celebration. Protecting your personal information and event details is a top priority.

Information We Collect

When you reach out through our website or inquiry forms, we collect details necessary to discuss and coordinate your celebration, including your name, partner's name, email address, phone number, event date, guest count estimate, event location, and celebration vision.

How We Use Your Information

All information provided is used exclusively to communicate with you regarding your wedding or event, prepare customized proposals, and deliver professional planning and coordination services. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

Client Privacy & Photography

We respect client privacy regarding event photos and media. Wedding day photographs and media are shared only with prior client permission and courtesy to the creative team involved.
