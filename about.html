THE PERSON BEHIND THE PLANNING

A little more about the
person by your side.

At Brides Right Hand, our conviction is simple: you deserve to be fully present on one of the most meaningful days of your life.

Christina Haydanek is the owner and lead planner behind Brides Right Hand, partnering with couples across Massachusetts, New England, and destination celebrations. With a grounded, attentive, and deeply personal approach, she steps in as your dedicated right hand—managing logistics, coordinating with your vendor team, and safeguarding your peace of mind so you can focus entirely on celebrating.

From timeline curation and vendor alignment to thoughtful day-of problem solving, our role is to absorb the logistical weight of the event, giving you and your family the freedom to make memories without distraction.

01 / OUR PHILOSOPHY A little less worry. A little more wonder.

Your wedding should
feel like yours.

A wedding is an intimate milestone, not an endurance test of logistical coordination. Too often, couples spend months creating a thoughtful vision only to find themselves fielding vendor inquiries, tracking timelines, and worrying over operational details when the day finally arrives.

Our philosophy is built on the belief that thorough preparation beforehand is what unlocks total presence in the moment. When every detail has been anticipated and given a dedicated caretaker, stress gives way to wonder, and your celebration unfolds with natural ease.

We believe in quiet luxury, intentional design, and genuine human connection. Your celebration should reflect your personal story, your aesthetic, and your peace of mind.
